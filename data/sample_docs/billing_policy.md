# Billing Policy

## Invoice schedule

Invoices are sent on the 1st of each month for all active subscriptions.
Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Late payments

Accounts with invoices overdue by more than 45 days may be suspended until
the balance is paid in full.

## Refunds

Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the charge.
Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
